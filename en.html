Sources and device data

VideoHub recognises entered or shared links locally and checks the clipboard on launch or resume. When you request formats or a download, the source and its servers receive the link and IP address. Engine update checks contact the distributor. Processing is necessary for the requested service (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

You sign in on the platform in the in-app browser. If you allow session use for a link, the engine uses platform cookies and writes a temporary copy in private cache, removed when the operation ends. You can decline and clear sessions in Settings.

Files, titles, sizes, local addresses and unlocks are managed on the device. Older files are recovered under Android media permissions. Sharing gives access to the recipient you choose. Clearing app data removes preferences and the acceptance receipt (version, language and time). Shared media may survive uninstall. Android backup can restore language and theme.

Appodeal and BidMachine may receive IP addresses, advertising or device identifiers, device and network details, usage, diagnostics and ad interactions for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. You can refuse and use the app. Advertising privacy options allow you to change or withdraw consent for future processing. Accepting terms and earning rewards are separate choices.
