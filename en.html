VideoHub · Android privacy notice

14 November 2026 · Test version

Who handles your data

Fudus OÜ operates VideoHub and is the controller for processing whose purposes and means it determines. Its Estonian registry code is 17357232. For questions, data requests or deletion of information sent to support, write to support@fudus.net or Fudus OÜ, Türi tn 10-18, Kesklinna linnaosa, 11313 Tallinn, Estonia.

Links, sources and sign-in sessions

When you paste or share a link and ask the app to inspect or download an item, your phone contacts the source and the servers that deliver the content. They receive the request and your connection’s IP address. This processing is necessary to carry out the access and saving you requested.

If a source requires sign-in, you enter your credentials on the platform’s website in the in-app browser. The platform handles authentication under its own privacy notice. The browser keeps the session on your phone. If you agree for the selected link, the engine can inspect or download the item using cookies from the session for that platform. A temporary copy is written to the app’s private cache and the app requests its removal when the operation ends. You can clear local app data in Android settings.

Files and information on your phone

The files you choose are saved in your phone’s media folders. The library shows media saved by your installation of VideoHub. In versions that support recovering earlier downloads, you can select a VideoHub folder using the Android folder picker. The app remembers your choice on your device and lists supported media from that folder without copying the files or sending them to Fudus. The library uses information such as file title, type, size and location. Hiding an item changes the library view. You can delete downloads from your current installation in the app; files recovered with read-only access stay in their original folder. When you share a file, the app or recipient you select receives it. Media in shared storage may remain on your device after you uninstall VideoHub.

Android backup can restore your language and theme if you enable it. To remember your acceptance of the terms, the app stores the version, language and time of acceptance locally. To let you retry a download, your device stores an unlock associated with an identifier derived from the link, format and selected item. The unlock is consumed when the file is saved. Acceptance records and unlocks are excluded from the settings backup. Clearing app data removes these local records.

To import an existing file, select it in the Android file picker. VideoHub retains local read access. The file stays in its original location.

Advertising and optional choices

VideoHub integrates Appodeal with BidMachine for ads. Depending on the configuration, applicable choices and type of request, the SDK and active partners may process your IP address, device or advertising identifiers, technical device and network information, app use, diagnostics and interactions with ads. They use this information to select and deliver ads, measure impressions and performance, and prevent fraud. Partners may operate outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. For their processing, recipients, retention and transfers, consult their notices or ask Fudus OÜ about applicable safeguards.

Where consent is required, you choose whether to allow personalisation in the advertising privacy form. You can refuse and continue using VideoHub. You can review and change your choices using the advertising privacy options available in your region. Withdrawing consent applies to subsequent processing. Accepting the terms and watching a video to earn an unlock are separate choices.

Appodeal: https://appodeal.com/privacy-policy/

BidMachine: https://www.bidmachine.com/privacy-policy

Support, diagnostics and retention

The local log keeps up to 60 technical entries on a rolling basis and is excluded from backup. When asking for help, you can review the last 24 entries and decide whether to prepare a draft in your email app. You can add the relevant link yourself. Only if you send the message will the support mailbox receive your email address and the details you included, via the email services involved.

Fudus OÜ processes your request to provide the support you asked for. To find out whether we hold a report, request its deletion or learn the period applicable to your case, contact support@fudus.net. Provide only the details needed to locate your message. Please omit passwords, cookies and identity documents from your initial request.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion or restriction of processing of your data. Where applicable, you may object to processing, request data portability or withdraw consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. Use the contact details above to exercise your rights. You can complain to the competent data protection authority, including where you usually live or work, or to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate, Andmekaitse Inspektsioon.
