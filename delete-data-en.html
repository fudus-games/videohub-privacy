How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@fudus.net (VideoHub - Data deletion request).
  2. Describe the data you want deleted, such as a previous support request. Do not send passwords, cookies or private links.
  3. Fudus will review the request and reply by email. We may request only the information needed to verify your identity.

Data stored on your device

In-app browser sessions, settings, history and technical logs can be removed through the app and Android settings. Media saved in shared storage may remain after uninstalling the app and must be deleted separately by you.

Support data and advertising partners

Fudus can delete identifiable support emails and voluntarily supplied data where applicable. Appodeal, BidMachine and other advertising partners may process data independently: use the app's advertising privacy settings or contact the partners. Fudus can help identify the appropriate contact.

Retention and exceptions

Local data remains on the device until you delete it. Support correspondence may be retained as needed to handle the request and for applicable legal obligations, security or disputes. No fixed additional retention period applies to local data. Our reply will explain what was deleted and what, if anything, must be retained and why. Fudus does not control data held by external platforms.
